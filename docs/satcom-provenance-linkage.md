## Registry linkage

`build_admitted_seventh_arc_l04_linkage()` constructs a deterministic registry
snapshot containing:

- one `DERIVED` and `ADMITTED` record for the benchmark fixture;
- one `VALIDATION` and `ADMITTED` record for the L0.4 output.

The fixture record preserves exact references to the already-frozen BTO-to-WGS84
transform and canonical sampling contract. Those upstream domain records remain
the scientific authority; #9D does not duplicate or reinterpret them.

The validation-output record declares the admitted fixture as its exact input and
preserves the merged L0.4 implementation and point-matching configuration as the
operation boundary.

## Attribution linkage

The L0.4 context records three separate relationships to the exact fixture:

```text
RETRIEVED  fixture available to the validation context
CITED      fixture_sha256 field in the validation output
USED       fixture consumed by the deterministic comparison computation
```

The evidence-use record is classified as `COMPUTATION`. It does not imply BFO
analysis, aircraft dynamics, probability, ranking, endpoint selection, or a
location claim.
